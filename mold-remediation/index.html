Treat mold as a warning as well as a health hazard, because the hidden moisture feeding it puts your property's structural integrity at risk. Breathing it in may lead to allergy symptoms, respiratory problems, headaches and more serious effects, and the risk is highest for the elderly, the immune-compromised and children. At Wesley Chapel Restoration, our IICRC-certified mold remediation technicians follow strict industry protocols to safely contain, remove and prevent mold in Wesley Chapel homes and commercial properties.

Real remediation involves a great deal more than cleaning visible growth from a wall. Done professionally, the work includes finding where the moisture comes from, containing the area so spores cannot cross-contaminate other rooms, taking out affected material under HEPA filtration, applying antimicrobials to what remains and correcting the moisture issue to prevent a return. Our comprehensive approach means mold is eliminated at the source, not just hidden for a while.

Each project starts with a careful inspection, and in many cases we test the air to learn which mold spores are present and at what concentration. This scientific approach lets us develop a targeted remediation plan for your specific situation. While the work is underway, the area stays under negative air pressure, HEPA scrubbers clean the air and our crew follows IICRC S520, the mold remediation standard. Afterward we verify the results, with clearance air testing if you request it, to confirm the property is safe to occupy.

Maintenance Tips for Mold Remediation

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly mold issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • When windows, piping or other cold surfaces start to sweat, correct it without waiting
  • Clear gutters and downspouts regularly, which keeps rain from soaking the foundation walls
  • Make sure bathrooms, kitchens and laundry areas have adequate exhaust ventilation
  • Regular filter changes and HVAC maintenance help keep mold out of the ducts
  • Hang or launder wet fabrics promptly and wipe up spills as they happen
  • Make sure crawl spaces are properly ventilated and consider installing a vapor barrier

Why Hire a Professional for Mold Remediation?

This is not a job to take on yourself. Disturbing mold colonies without proper containment sends millions of spores airborne, which can contaminate unaffected areas of your home and create worse health exposure than the original problem. Our crews work in personal protective equipment inside a negative-pressure enclosure, with HEPA air scrubbers running, to trap spores and hold them in the work area while removal is underway.

Professional mold remediation also addresses the root cause of the growth, which is the moisture source. Clean off the visible growth but leave the moisture problem, and the mold comes back, in many cases in a matter of weeks. Certified remediation technicians have the training and equipment to identify hidden moisture sources, dry affected areas properly and put solutions in place that prevent recurrence.

How to Tell You Need Mold Remediation

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Recurring respiratory symptoms, allergies or headaches that improve when you are away from home
  • Long-running hidden leaks, for example at a dishwasher line, sink drain or roof flashing
  • Dark staining or discoloration on walls, ceilings or around HVAC vents
  • Belongings in storage, including clothes and cardboard, showing spots or fuzz
  • Warping, blistered paint or wallpaper lifting off the wall, all clues to moisture you cannot see
  • Water damage not long ago that was left to dry without professional equipment
  • Visible mold growth of any color on walls, ceilings, flooring or other surfaces
