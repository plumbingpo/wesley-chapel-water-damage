Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
  • Check window and door seals each year and replace any caulking that has cracked
  • Learn where the main water shutoff is and how to close it if pipes are damaged in a storm
  • Clogged gutters overflow in a downpour, so clear them and the downspouts often
  • Photograph your property once a year so its condition is on record for insurance purposes
  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation

When to Call for Storm Damage Repair in Wesley Chapel

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Flooding in lower levels because drainage systems were overwhelmed
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Wind-driven rain coming in through windows, doors or wall penetrations
  • New brown rings or damp marks on walls and ceilings once the storm passes
  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Damaged or displaced flashing around roof penetrations, valleys or edges
